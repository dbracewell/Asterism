# Troubleshooting

Messages you might see in Asterism, what they mean and what to do. If none of
these help, ask your administrator. Administrators can also check
**Admin Settings** › **Status** and the [deployment guide](../deployment.md).

## Signing in

**"This account is suspended. Ask your administrator."**
Your administrator has blocked your account. Nothing has been deleted. Ask them
to reactivate it.

**The sign-in page says your email or password is wrong.**
Check the spelling and that Caps Lock is off. If you've forgotten your password,
ask your administrator for a
[password reset link](01-getting-started/signing-in.md#set-a-new-password).

**"This invite can't be used."** or **"This reset link can't be used."**
The link has been used already, has expired or was withdrawn. Ask for a new one:
an invite from your educator, a reset link from your administrator.

**"Too many sign-up attempts. Try again in a few minutes."**
Wait a few minutes, then try again.

## Chatting

**"Chat capacity is full. Retry shortly."**
Asterism is answering as many chats as it can at once. Wait a minute and send
your message again. If you have many Asterism tabs open, close the ones you
aren't using. Administrators can see how busy Asterism is in
**Admin Settings** › **Status**.

**"You've used your daily budget of … tokens. It resets …"** (or **monthly**)
Your group has a limit on how much each person can use, and you've reached it.
New messages, regenerated answers, study tools and images wait until the time
shown. You can see your usage in
[Settings › Your Asterism](06-making-it-yours/about-you-and-usage.md#usage).
Administrators set budgets in
[Groups](08-for-administrators/groups-budgets-and-usage.md#set-a-usage-budget).

**"Too many requests. Wait a minute and try again."**
You (or your school's network) sent a lot of requests in a short time, for
example many uploads at once. Wait a minute, then carry on.

**"Selected model is unavailable; choose …"**, or a model shows
**Model unavailable**
The administrator has turned that model off, or your group can't use it any more.
Choose another model in the
[model chip](02-chatting/choosing-and-comparing-models.md). For a sub-agent, edit
it and choose another model.

**An answer is marked Failed.**
Something went wrong while it was written. Send your message again. If it keeps
failing, try another model, or ask your administrator.

**The chat says it isn't connected.**
Open the ring beside the send button: **Connection** should say **Connected**. If
it doesn't, check your internet connection and reload the page.

## Files

**A file says Embedding: failed.**
Asterism couldn't read the file. Select the circular arrow to
[try again](03-files-and-projects/the-files-library.md#try-again-after-a-failure).
If it fails again, the file is probably damaged or empty: open it on your
computer, then upload a good copy.

**A file is still processing after a long time.**
Large files take longer, and files wait their turn when many are uploaded at
once. Reload the page after a few minutes. Administrators can see waiting files
in **Admin Settings** › **Status**.

**Asterism doesn't seem to use my file.**
Check [Files in context](02-chatting/attaching-files.md#see-which-files-are-in-context):
the file must be listed and ready. Then ask about it by name.

## Features that are missing

If a button or setting in this guide isn't on your screen, your administrator
may not have turned that feature on, or your group may not be allowed to use it.
This applies to skills, the canvas, image creation, sub-agents, some tools and
ChatGPT plans. Ask your administrator.
