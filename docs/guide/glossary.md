# Glossary

Words you will see in Asterism, in plain language. The chapters link here the
first time they use a term.

**Administrator (admin).** The person who runs your Asterism: they connect the AI
models, manage accounts and decide what each group may use.

**Attachment.** A file you add to one chat, such as your notes or a photo of
your work, so Asterism can read it.

**Branch.** Another version of an answer. When you ask Asterism to try again, the
new answer is kept next to the old one, and the arrows under the answer switch
between them.

**Canvas.** A panel beside the chat for a longer piece of work, such as an essay
outline or some code. You and Asterism can both edit it, and every saved version
is kept.

**Chat.** A conversation with Asterism. Chats are saved, so you can come back to
them.

**Citation.** A small number in an answer that shows which of your files a
sentence came from. Select it to see the passage.

**Class.** A group of students run by an educator. Your educator can share course
material with the class, and it shows up as a project in your sidebar.

**Class code.** A short code from your educator. If you already have an account,
you join a class by entering it under **Classes**.

**Compare models.** Sending the same message to two or three models and seeing
their answers side by side.

**Context compaction.** When a chat gets very long, Asterism summarizes the
earlier messages so the chat keeps working. Your messages are never deleted.

**Educator.** Someone who can create classes and share course material with
students.

**Files in context.** The files Asterism may use in a chat: the project's files
plus the chat's own attachments.

**General chat.** A chat that isn't part of a project.

**Group.** Everyone belongs to one group, such as student or educator. Your group
decides which models and tools you can use.

**Invite link.** A link from an educator that creates your account and puts you
in their class. Each link works once.

**Learning mode.** How Asterism helps you in a chat. In Standard mode it answers
normally; in Tutor mode it guides you with questions and hints instead.

**Main agent.** The assistant that answers your chats. You can give it
instructions and choose its tools in Settings.

**Model.** The AI that writes the answers. Your administrator decides which models
you can choose from.

**Password reset link.** A link from your administrator that lets you choose a
new password. It works once.

**Project.** A place for one subject or piece of work. A project keeps its own
chats, files and study material together.

**Prompt coach.** A review of your draft message before you send it, with
suggestions you can apply.

**Prompt template.** A form that builds a clear message from a few fields, such
as role, task and format.

**Sidebar.** The column down the left side of the screen with **New Chat**, your
chats and projects, and your name. On a phone, open it with the button at the top
left.

**Skill.** Instructions you wrote once and can switch on in a chat by typing
`/`. A skill adds instructions only; it doesn't give Asterism new tools or
files.

**Source.** One of your files that Asterism read to answer a question. Sources are
listed above the answer.

**Tool.** An action Asterism can take while answering, such as searching your
files or reading a web page.

**Tool approval.** A tool action that waits for you to select Approve or Deny
before it runs.
